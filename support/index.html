Support

Questions, problems, or feedback about BookLyre or BookLyre Studio? Email support@booklyre.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

New here? Start with the guides.

You can also send a report from inside the app: tap Spotted a bug? and you can review everything before it's sent.

Common questions

Which books can I use?

DRM-free EPUB files. BookLyre can't open books protected by DRM, which includes most books bought from the big ebook stores. Many authors and publishers sell DRM-free editions, and public-domain books are DRM-free.

Does my book get uploaded anywhere?

No. Your books are analysed and their audio is generated on your device. We don't run servers that receive your books, and we can't see them. See the privacy policy.

How much space does it need?

About 1 GB for the app and its built-in voice model, plus space for the audio it prepares. You can see and delete each book's audio in Settings → Storage.

My device gets warm while generating.

Generating audio is demanding work. The app slows down automatically when your device gets hot. Keeping it plugged in and somewhere cool helps.

How do I restore a purchase?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Open the app's purchase screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

How do I cancel a BookLyre Studio subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose BookLyre Studio, and tap Cancel Subscription. Your library and the audio you've already made stay on your device.
