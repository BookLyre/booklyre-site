Getting started with BookLyre Studio

BookLyre Studio helps independent authors turn their own books into full-cast audiobooks, all on an iPhone or iPad. Generate a cast of AI voices, record your own narration, and export a finished audiobook for your readers.

1. Start a project

  1. Choose your role when you first open the app: Author to produce your books, Narrator to record them, or Reader to listen. You can switch any time in Settings.
  2. Tap New Project and import your manuscript as an EPUB.
  3. Confirm that the book is your own work and you hold the exclusive rights to it. Studio is for works you own. If a file looks like a published edition from another publisher, add it to your Reader library for personal listening instead.

Your first author project is free.

2. Review the cast

Studio detects the characters and assigns each line of dialogue to a speaker.

  • Open Characters to see everyone, their line counts and their voices. Tap a character to change their voice and preview it.
  • Use Auto-Assign or Batch Assign to set voices quickly, and Clean Up to merge duplicate names.
  • Fix any line that went to the wrong character, and add pronunciations for names and invented words.
  • Export Cast Script gives you a script of every character's lines, which is useful for human narrators.

Detection is automatic and won't be perfect, so always listen through before you publish.

3. Generate the audiobook

Tap Generate Audiobook. Studio works through four steps:

  1. Prepare chapters
  2. Confirm the cast: one last look at who sounds like whom
  3. Render chapter audio
  4. Package the audiobook

Generation runs fastest with the app open and your device plugged in somewhere cool. If it stops, start it again: finished chapters are kept and it picks up at the next one. Each finished audiobook needs extra storage, so check you have space first.

4. Record your own narration (optional)

In Narrator Studio you can record a character's lines one at a time, re-take any line, and track progress per character. You can also create a voice from recordings of your own voice and use it in generation. Manage those under Settings → Voice & Audio → My Voices. You may only make a voice from your own recordings.

5. Export

Studio offers two audio exports:

  • Audiobook (M4B): one file with chapter markers, cover art and book details, mastered for listening.
  • Retail Audio Pack: one mastered file per section with room tone, opening and closing credits, a retail sample, and a report showing whether every file meets the ACX audio limits.

Add your Narrated by credit and opening and closing credits, and choose whether chapter titles are announced. Before exporting, Studio asks you to confirm you own the rights to the work. When you publish, follow your store's rules on disclosing synthetic narration.

You can also export the manuscript itself as EPUB, PDF, Word, Markdown or plain text.

6. Sync and collaborate

Turn on iCloud sync in Settings → Collaboration → iCloud Sync to keep your library details, progress and settings in step across your devices. Book files, recordings and audio stay on each device, so keep your own backups. You can also share a project through iCloud with beta readers, narrators or editors.

7. Plans

One author project is free. Author (annual or lifetime) adds production EPUB and audio exports and more projects. If a subscription ends, everything you've made stays on your device. Manage or cancel in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device.
